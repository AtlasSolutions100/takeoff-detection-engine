How it works

  1. 1

    Upload a plan set

    Upload the PDF plan set you are bidding, then open a sheet.

  2. 2

    Detect - lines, symbols, scale

    Detect finds every line family on the sheet and measures it, and reads the scale from the printed words and the drawn bar. If they disagree, or the sheet prints several scales, it tells you instead of guessing. Counts then scans the sheet for repeated symbols.

  3. 3

    Review, price and export

    Check each quantity against the drawing, then press Add to takeoff or Approve. Your unit price book prices the rows. Export an itemized CSV, a report PDF of every approved sheet, or a bid-sheet CSV.

What it reads

Each tool adds its rows to the same takeoff.

Cut / fill
Existing and proposed contours and spot grades, turned into cut and fill volumes. Cut shows red, fill shows blue, and there is a 3D view.
Units: CY
Trenching
Pick a pipe run and set its depths. You get the trench dig and the bedding, with the pipe footage split into depth bands.
Units: CY · LF
Counts
Repeated symbols - windows, doors, fixtures - counted and checked against the sheet's own schedule.
Units: EA
Flooring
Rooms grouped by floor type, with the wall base around each room.
Units: SF · LF
Linework
Every line family on a sheet, found and measured. One grading sheet: 1,429 runs in 113 line styles, every one carrying the drafter's own layer name.
Units: LF
Structures
Outline a building and type the depths. You get the dig, the backfill and the slab prep, plus the drain run around the foundation.
Units: CY · SF · LF
