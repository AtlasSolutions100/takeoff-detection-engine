Lines

Datum

Two kinds of short number. A project datum: the sheet prints 87 or 100.5 with a note such as "100.00 = 8800.00" (type it above). Truncated elevations: the sheet prints 48.07 for 7,748.07; these are resolved from the sheet's own heights and say so, or type the base they add.

Building pads

A floor height printed inside a closed outline. Turn one on and the proposed ground is flat at that height over the outline (hover to see it). Turn it on only where the outline is the building: on a lot plan the outline round a floor height is the lot. A layer stack lowers a pad with the rest of the site.

Limit

The limit gives the fence length, the Clear & Grub area and the strip area (Takeoff rows card). Pick the sheet's own limit line, or click corners on the sheet and double-click (or Enter) to close. Without a limit the overlap of the two surfaces is used.

Layer stack under the proposed grade

Asphalt, base, gravel: the drawing shows finished grade, the dig stops that much lower. The stack's total lowers the proposed surface inside the limit (volumes to subgrade). Empty = finished grade.

Grading zones

Split the job the way a bid form does (streets, lots, drainage): each zone is dug to its own depth and files its own rows in the takeoff. With no limit drawn, the zones are the area; with a limit, the rest of it is its own row. "Show only" puts one zone alone on the cut / fill map.

Sheet alignment

Evidence from other sheets is mapped into the sheet shown. Same-size sheets from one set usually line up as-is; pick two landmarks on each sheet if they do not. The Overlay shows another sheet under this one, placed the way the volumes use it. Its contour lines are dashed and faint; fix them on their own sheet.
