Earthwork — assign contours

How to do this
  1. Each row is one contour line. Click a row and the line lights up on the sheet.
  2. Ask: is this a real contour? If not, press × to drop it.
  3. Pick a side. EXIST is the ground now. PROP is the new ground. skip means do not use it.
  4. Is the number right? If not, type the right one.
  5. Draw a work area, or set the limit of work, so only that part counts.
  6. Press Calculate cut & fill.
What this number is. We make one surface from the EXIST lines and one from the PROP lines. Then we take one away from the other. That is a guess made from the lines. It is not a measured dig.
What it cost once. We checked it on one job (Beaumont Barn C3.0, printed sheet 3) against the real Estimate #853. Our cut, fill and import were each 2.3 to 2.5 times too high. The area it used was 1.42 times too big (317,632 SF against 224,058 SF). That was one job, not a rule. Treat every number below as a rough check. A person still has to do the real one.
Where it counts. Each surface only reaches as far as its own lines. The volume is counted where the two surfaces overlap. That shape is a straight-sided box around the lines, so it is not the real graded area. Set a limit of work below and it will be.
What it leaves out. The road section, the dig depth under each surface, and how much dirt swells or shrinks. None of the three is on the plans or in this number.

Pipe trench

Pick the pipe run, set its size and invert depths, and adjust the bedding rules to the spec. Depth interpolates start→end along each line; trench width = pipe OD + clearance each side, growing with depth per the rules. Sides are vertical unless you lay them back: a 1:1 layback adds a V of depth × depth per foot of run (a 6 ft trench adds 36 SF of section), and it goes to backfill, not bedding. Water and sewer here run about 6 ft and are laid back; gas and electric usually are not — the Utility box fills those in.

Structure — dig & backfill

Outline the building, then type the depths. Mass Excavation is the whole footprint dug to the slab (footprint × depth). Structure Excavation is the trench outside the foundation wall, dug to the bottom of the footing with the sides laid back for safety: per foot of wall it is depth × (footing width + half the depth at 1:1). A 6 ft dig with a 2 ft footing is 6 × (2 + 3) = 30 SF of section. Structure Backfill is the same section figured from the footing bottom up to the proposed grade, which is not always the ground you dug from — leave fill depth blank and it is assumed equal to the dig, and the row says so. Slab Prep is the footprint area. All volumes in cubic yards (÷ 27). Two lineal rows come off the same outline: Perimeter Drain is the length around the outside of the foundation, and Radon is the inside face of the exterior walls (the outline pulled in by the wall thickness).
